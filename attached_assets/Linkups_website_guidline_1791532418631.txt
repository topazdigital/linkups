21. The visual identity
I'd push LinkUps toward:
Luxury + Adventure
Not overly luxurious like a five-star hotel.
Not overly rugged like a backpacking company.
Think:
African adventure + premium lifestyle + youthful energy.
Your existing brand colours should be carried throughout the website rather than introducing many new colours.
Typography

Large bold adventure-style headings
paired with
clean modern body text.
Lots of white/negative space.

Subtle animations.

Instagram ? Package page ? Book ? M-Pesa/payment ? Confirmation

Instagram ? Find Your Adventure ? Recommendations based on interests ? Package ? Book
CUSTOMER 3 — Wants something customized
Google/Instagram ? Plan My Adventure ? Enquiry form ? WhatsApp/email ? Customized quotation ? Deposit ? Confirmation
That covers most of your business.

27. The technology I'd recommend
For LinkUps, you don't need something unnecessarily complicated at first.
A good setup would be:
WordPress + WooCommerce/booking system
or
Webflow + booking/enquiry integration
If you're going to manage packages yourself frequently, I'd lean toward WordPress because it gives you more flexibility and generally makes it easier to manage a growing travel catalogue.
Eventually you can integrate:
M-Pesa payments
WhatsApp
